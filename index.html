Can I swap deliverables?

List what you need in your brief. For example, a trades business might ask to swap the flyer for a truck decal design. The final proposal confirms the scope, price and timeline.

What counts as a revision round?

A round is one set of consolidated feedback on the work in scope. New deliverables or a change in approved direction are quoted separately.
